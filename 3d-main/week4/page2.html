Technology Field Guide

Weeks 1–4

CS110 Course Recap and Learning Reflection

This field guide summarizes the major concepts, frameworks, and technical skills introduced during the first four weeks of CS110. The course emphasized personal development, web development, storytelling, collaboration, and the integration of technology with creative communication.

Week 1

Mindset and Support

Week 1 focused on developing a productive mindset and recognizing the importance of personal support throughout the learning process.

  • Develop a growth-oriented mindset rather than approaching challenges with a fixed mindset.
  • Become your own advocate and recognize your role as your first source of encouragement and motivation.
  • Understand the importance of using available tools, resources, and teammates to overcome challenges.
  • Review the foundational roles of web technologies: HTML provides the structure, CSS controls presentation and visual design, and JavaScript adds interactivity and responsiveness.

Week 2

Web Development

Week 2 introduced a broader understanding of web development and emphasized the value of collaboration and modern development tools.

  • Use artificial intelligence as a development aid to research ideas, generate code, troubleshoot problems, and improve understanding.
  • Recognize collaboration as an important component of successful web development.
  • Develop a stronger understanding of web-development frameworks and how they organize and support applications.
  • Connect individual technical skills with larger development workflows and project goals.

Week 3

Storytelling

Week 3 explored storytelling as a structured method for communicating ideas effectively through digital experiences.

  • Examine storytelling frameworks such as AAA, SEEK, and PEEK.
  • Apply the AAA framework: Attention, Action, and Amplification.
  • Use SEEK as a framework for organizing and preparing a storyteller's outline.
  • Understand how structured storytelling can help communicate information clearly and maintain audience engagement.

Week 4

Enhanced Storytelling Frameworks

Week 4 connected storytelling principles with interactive web development and introduced the use of 3D technology to strengthen digital communication.

  • Explore the PEEK storytelling framework and its application to digital communication.
  • Apply a storyteller's outline to the planning and development of a website.
  • Connect visual design and storytelling to create more meaningful digital experiences.
  • Use BabylonJS to create a 3D object or shape that represents an idea, concept, or element of a story within a website.
